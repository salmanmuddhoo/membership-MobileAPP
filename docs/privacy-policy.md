# Privacy policy — Al Barakah member app

_Al Barakah Multi-purpose Co-operative Society Ltd ("the Society", "we")._
_Last updated: 7 October 2026._

This policy explains what the Al Barakah member app on your phone collects,
why, and what happens to it. It is to be published at a public address and
linked from the app's Google Play listing.

## Who we are

Al Barakah Multi-purpose Co-operative Society Ltd, Mauritius. For anything
about this policy or your data, contact the office on +230 5944 9797.

## What the app collects

**To sign you in.** Your NIC and AB Number, used once to find your
membership record; a one-time code sent to the mobile number on that
record. The NIC and AB Number are not stored on the phone. The app keeps a
session token on the phone, in its secure storage, so you do not have to
enter them again.

**To show you your membership.** Your name, Member No., the details on your
membership record, your accounts and their balances and transactions, and,
if you are the guardian of a minor member, that minor's accounts. These are
read from the Society's records when you open the app and are not kept on
the phone beyond the session.

**If you apply for membership from the app.** The details you type on the
application form and any document you photograph or attach (identity card,
proof of address). Photos are taken only when you choose to attach one.

**On the phone only.** A PIN you choose, kept as a salted digest in the
phone's secure storage, never sent to us. Whether you have chosen to hide
balances. Nothing else.

**We do not collect** your location, contacts, call logs, other apps, or
anything from your phone beyond what is listed here. The app shows no
third-party advertising and contains no analytics or tracking library.

## Why

To let you see your membership and accounts, apply for membership, and use
your membership card at partner outlets. Processing rests on your
membership agreement with the Society and, for an application, on your
request to join.

## Who sees it

The Society's staff, as they do for any member. The backend runs on Vercel,
which processes the data on our behalf under its own commitments. Nothing
is sold or shared with any other party. Partner outlets see your card, not
your data.

## How long

Session tokens expire after ninety days without use and are revoked when
you sign out. Details on your membership record are kept for as long as the
Society's records require. You may ask the office to delete your account
on the app at any time; the membership record itself is governed by the
Society's rules.

## Security

Data travels only over encrypted connections. On the phone, tokens and the
PIN live in secure storage; the app refuses screenshots while you are
signed in and locks after a short time in the background. Five wrong PINs
sign the phone out.

## Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
and withdraw from the app at any time, by contacting the office. Under the
Data Protection Act 2017 of Mauritius you may also complain to the Data
Protection Office.

## Changes

We will update this page and the date above when anything here changes.
